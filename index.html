00 / The premise

An order isn't complete when it arrives.
It's complete when it can be trusted.

In a distributor-to-ERP workflow, a seemingly small difference in product, units, price or purchase-order identity can become a delayed shipment, duplicate invoice or incorrect stock movement.

This independent exercise follows an illustrative EDI 850 purchase order through a simulated ERP validation process. The aim: demonstrate how a junior systems analyst could investigate, document and hand off an exception.

01 / CONTAIN

Protect the operation

Hold suspect records, identify the business impact, preserve source messages and avoid unauthorized overrides.

02 / CONFIRM

Find the source of truth

Validate with the appropriate data owner, integration analyst or business stakeholder.

03 / PROVE

Test the correction

Reconcile fields and amounts; cover positive, negative and regression scenarios in UAT.

04 / COMMUNICATE

Make the handoff usable

Record approval, accountable owner, test evidence, downstream impact and next steps in the support record.
